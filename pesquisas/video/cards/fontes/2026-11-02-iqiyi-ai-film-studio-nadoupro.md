# Reviewed evidence: iQIYI AI Film Studio and NadouPro (2026-11-02)

## Timeline
- 2026-10-28: iQIYI began filming "Cop in Trouble" at the newly opened AI Film Studio in Shanghai.
- 2026-11-02: iQIYI press release made the studio deployment public. This is a production-workflow announcement, not a new foundation-model release.

## Verified capabilities and availability
- The approximately 110-square-meter physical studio records multi-camera live-action footage, high-resolution video/audio and digital continuity metadata.
- Material flows to NadouPro for postproduction with close to 30 integrated AI models, including Seedance 2.5 and Kling 4.0, and hundreds of production agents and skills.
- The goal is to preserve actors' performances while modifying sets, lighting, costumes and other visual elements in postproduction.
- Studio status: operational for iQIYI production, not a generally bookable public studio.
- NadouPro status: active professional platform with credit-based commercial arrangements; publicly documented product offerings cover scripts, video generation, editing and commercial licensing. Public third-party API availability and per-second prices are not confirmed.
- Models integrated in NadouPro retain their own provider-specific operational, licensing and commercial restrictions; the studio announcement does not give unrestricted model access or open weights.

## Evidence limitations
- "China's first AI Film Studio" is iQIYI's own description, not an independently verified first.
- The claimed over-30% production efficiency gain concerns an earlier Hengdian virtual studio, NOT a measured result from the new Shanghai facility or the film "Cop in Trouble".
- No independent head-to-head quality study, production budget, release date, external studio booking terms or published API SLA was identified.
